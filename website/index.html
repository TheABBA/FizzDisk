What FizzDisk does

  • Blu-ray / DVD — reads the disc (incl. AACS-protected discs), picks the movie, and makes an MKV with your chosen audio/subtitle tracks. FFmpeg is built in.
  • Audio CDs — rips to FLAC and/or MP3 with paranoia error correction, automatic MusicBrainz titles and cover art.
  • Fully offline — movies are identified via an optional free TheMovieDB key; everything else works with no account.
