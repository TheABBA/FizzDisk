FizzDisk is fully functional offline, but when you scan or rip a disc it may reach the internet to identify the disc and produce richer output:

  • Disc metadata & cover art. The Software may contact third-party public web services (MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, and optionally TheMovieDB) using a disc identifier — a small value derived from your disc's table of contents, track timing, volume id or title. In doing so, the titles of the discs you rip may be transmitted to and read by those public metadata services. Your audio/video files themselves are never uploaded — only the identifiers and titles used to look up metadata.

  • No ads, no tracking, no sale of data. We do not run ad services, sell your data, or store your media. Only the identifiers described above leave your machine.

If you are offline the Software continues to work; only the metadata lookup and online activation are skipped until you reconnect.
