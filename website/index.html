Clean-room parsers

Hand-written DVD IFO (TT_SRPT/VTS_PGCIT) and Blu-ray MPLS playlist parsers scan the disc structure from scratch — no other project's parser code.

Full stream inspection

Video, audio and subtitle tracks per title with codec, language, resolution and quality — so you know exactly what you're backing up.

⚡

Lossless MKV backup

Stream-copy by default: fast backups, zero quality loss. Uncheck the box to re-encode (H.264 / x264 / AAC) when you want smaller files.

🎵

CDs perfected

CDs rip to FLAC and/or MP3 with paranoia error correction, automatic MusicBrainz titles and cover art — a clean copy, every time.

Compress as you rip

Encode your video (H.264 / x264) and audio (AAC, MP3, FLAC) right during the rip — every encoder is bundled, so you get much smaller files with no additional software to install.
